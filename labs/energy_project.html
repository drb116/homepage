Energy Conversion Design Challenge

Design, test, and improve a warning device that changes electrical energy into light, sound, or motion.

Your Challenge

A bicycle rider needs a warning device that other people can notice. Build a device that creates a useful signal.

Criteria:
  • The circuit must be complete.
  • The device must create light, sound, or motion.
  • The signal strength must reach at least 60.
Constraints:
  • Use one power source.
  • Use one switch.
  • Use one output device.
  • Spend no more than 10 points.
