Human Vision

Humans are especially good at seeing color and fine detail in daylight. In darkness, we can eventually see shapes, but colors become difficult to identify.

Try These

Model note: Real vision depends on much more than the number of rods and cones. Eye size, pupil shape, reflective tissues, receptor arrangement, and brain processing also matter. The animal ratings here are simplified teaching categories.
