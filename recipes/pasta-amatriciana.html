Pasta Amatriciana

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 ounces guanciale or pancetta, chopped into ¼-inch cubes (¾ cup)
  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole tomatoes, crushed by hand in a bowl
  • ⅛ teaspoon black pepper, plus more to taste
  • ⅛ teaspoon red-pepper flakes
  • Kosher salt, such as Diamond Crystal
  • 1 pound dried bucatini
  • ¾ cup grated Pecorino Romano, plus more for garnish

Directions

  1. In a large (12-inch) skillet, heat the olive oil over medium. Add the guanciale and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, about 5 minutes. Remove the guanciale from the skillet and set it aside. Add the tomatoes, black pepper, and red-pepper flakes to the skillet, and season with salt. Cook, stirring occasionally and crushing the tomatoes with the back of a wooden spoon, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce has thickened, about 15 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in a large pot of salted water according to the package directions until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta cooking water, then drain.
  3. Add the pasta, tomato sauce, reserved guanciale, and ½ cup of the pasta cooking water back to the large pasta pot. Stir vigorously over medium-high heat until the pasta is evenly coated in the sauce, about 1 minute. Add more pasta water if the sauce is dry. Remove from the heat, stir in the cheese, and season to taste with salt.
  4. Divide the pasta among bowls and garnish with more cheese and black pepper.
