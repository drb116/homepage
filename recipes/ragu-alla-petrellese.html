Ragù alla Petrellese — Our Family Version

Ingredients

  • 1 pound (450 g) fresh mild Italian pork sausage, preferably links
  • 1 (28-ounce / 800 g) can whole San Marzano tomatoes
  • 2–3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 whole garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • Salt, as needed
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons fresh Italian parsley, finely chopped
  • Pecorino Romano, freshly grated
  • 500–600 g fresh cavatelli

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the whole garlic cloves and cook for 1–2 minutes without letting them darken. Add the sausage links whole and brown them gently on all sides.
  2. Step 2: Add the white wine and scrape the browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Simmer until most of the wine has evaporated.
  3. Step 3: Add the tomatoes and all their juice. Stir in the onion powder and several grinds of black pepper. Season lightly with salt.
  4. Step 4: Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer, then lower the heat. Cook partially covered for 60–90 minutes, stirring occasionally. If the sauce becomes too thick, add a small splash of water.
  5. Step 5: When the sauce is rich and the sausage is completely cooked, transfer the sausage links to a platter. Remove the garlic cloves, then taste the sauce and adjust the salt and pepper.
  6. Step 6: Cook the fresh cavatelli in well-salted water. Drain and toss with plenty of ragù. Finish with the chopped parsley and serve with freshly grated Pecorino Romano and the sausage alongside.

Comments

Traditionally, this would use an onion, but for our family the onion is replaced with onion powder.
