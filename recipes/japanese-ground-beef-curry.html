Japanese Ground Beef Curry

Yield: 4 to 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons sunflower, safflower, or canola oil
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • Salt and ground pepper
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 large garlic clove, minced
  • 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and minced
  • 1 large carrot, peeled and chopped into 1/3-inch pieces
  • 1 large russet potato, peeled and chopped into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 (3.2-ounce) package Japanese curry roux bricks, preferably medium-hot S&B Golden Curry
  • 3/4 cup frozen peas
  • Cooked rice, udon, or lo mein noodles, for serving

Directions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the oil. When it shimmers, add the ground beef, a generous pinch of salt, and a couple pinches of ground pepper. Break up the beef, spread it into an even layer, and cook undisturbed until browned on the bottom, about 3 minutes.
  2. Stir the beef and push it to one side of the skillet. Add the onion to the other side, season it lightly with salt, and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 2 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger, stir everything together, and cook for about 3 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the carrot and potato. Cook until their edges begin to soften, about 4 minutes.
  4. Add the curry roux bricks and 2 cups water. Stir, breaking up the bricks until they melt into a bubbling sauce. Cover, lower the heat to low, and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the liquid reduces to a rich, viscous sauce, about 15 minutes. If the skillet begins to dry out, gradually add up to 1/2 cup more water, a few tablespoons at a time, until the sauce is glossy and saucy.
  5. Add the peas and cook until bright green, 3 to 5 minutes.
  6. Serve over cooked rice, udon, or lo mein noodles. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days.

Original recipe: Japanese Ground Beef Curry, NYT Cooking
